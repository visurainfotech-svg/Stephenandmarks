Who we are

Two names.
One standard.

Stephen & Marks.Co is an editing studio built around one idea: a listing's first impression happens on a screen, long before anyone stands in the doorway. So that first impression has to hold up - colour-true, well paced, and true to how the space actually feels.

Send us your raw photos, video, or drone footage and we handle the rest - colour correction, sky replacement, cuts, floor plan drafting, and turning still photos into short, moving footage when there's no time or budget for a full video shoot.

What we edit and deliver

Four services, one editor - send raw files from any shoot and get back listing-ready media.

i.

Photo Editing

Colour correction, sky replacement, HDR blending and retouching - sized for MLS, print, and social.

Same-week delivery
ii.

Video Editing

Raw walkthrough and drone footage cut into cinematic property films with music, colour, and pacing.

Any footage source
iii.

Floor Plans

Clean, to-scale 2D and 3D plans drafted from your sketches, scans, or on-site measurements.

24h turnaround
iv.

Image to Video

Still photos brought to motion - pans, zooms and transitions built from images you already have.

No reshoot needed
v.

Virtual Staging

Empty or dated rooms furnished digitally - realistic furniture, decor and lighting added in post.

Empty room? No problem
